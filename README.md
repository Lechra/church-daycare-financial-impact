# Project: Revenue Diversification & Financial Sustainability Analysis
## Executive Decision Support for a Mid-Sized Organization

## Executive Summary

**The Problem**  
A mid-sized organization relying exclusively on voluntary donations (tithes and offerings) experienced high revenue volatility and limited growth capacity. This instability posed a risk to sustaining long-term community programs and operational planning.

**The Solution**  
I designed a three-year financial projection model comparing the existing *Donation-Only* revenue structure against a *Diversified Revenue* model that introduced a fee-based daycare program.

**The Result**  
The analysis revealed a clear path to predictable monthly cash flow, enabling leadership to move forward with confidence toward a phased daycare launch planned for 2026. The model provided data-backed clarity on revenue stability, growth potential, and operational risk.

---

## Strategic Insights

- **Volatility Mitigation**  
  Analysis of historical financial data (2022–2025) identified a 15–20% fluctuation in monthly income. Introducing daycare revenue establishes a stable baseline (“revenue floor”), significantly reducing month-to-month volatility.

- **Revenue Efficiency & Growth**  
  While projected membership growth remains modest, the 2026–2028 model shows a 35% increase in revenue per square foot under the diversified revenue scenario.

- **Risk Management Strategy**  
  A phased rollout was recommended to validate enrollment assumptions and demand before committing to full operational overhead, reducing financial exposure.

---

## My Consulting Process

- **Data Auditing & Preparation**  
  Cleaned, validated, and structured four years of historical financial and membership data using R.

- **Trend & Risk Analysis**  
  Visualized seasonal donation patterns to identify high-risk months where cash reserves were most vulnerable.

- **Predictive Modeling & Scenario Planning**  
  Built a three-year financial forecast using R, ggplot2, and R Markdown, enabling leadership to evaluate multiple “What-If” scenarios.

- **Executive Communication**  
  Delivered insights through an interactive, board-ready HTML report focused on decision clarity, not just metrics.
